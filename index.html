DOUBLE GEE HOTEL

Doubl Gee Hotel A cozy neighborhood spot, perfect for any occasion. This charming bistro combines classic comfort food with a modern twist, optionally served with your choice of seasonal sides, house-made sauces, or craft cocktails.

    Menu highlights
  • Pan-seared salmon
  • Salt, to taste
  • Optional sides: roasted vegetables, garlic mashed potatoes, artisan bread, fresh herbs
  1. Instructions Be seated: Greeted at the door, you'll be guided to a warm table by candlelight. You can request a booth near the window for a quieter experience
  2. Browse the menu: Settle in with a glass of water and freshly baked sourdough while you explore the day's specials chalkboard.
  3. Order your meal: Once the server has poured your drink of choice, place your order. Tilt the conversation toward the chef's recommendation for the freshest catch of the day.
  4. Savor and share: the courses arrive, carefully lift the lid off each plate and inhale. Let each bite linger for a moment, then pass the dishes around the table
  5. Enjoy: content, with a to-go box for dessert if needed.
